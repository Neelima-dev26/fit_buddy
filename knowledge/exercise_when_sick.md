# Can I exercise when I am sick?
Category: general
Keywords: sick, cold, flu, fever, illness, rest, return to exercise
Summary: Rest if you have a fever or symptoms below the neck, and ease back in afterward.

A common rule of thumb: if your symptoms are above the neck, such as a stuffy nose, light activity like walking may be fine. If you have a fever, body aches, chest congestion, or an upset stomach, rest.

When you feel better, return gradually at a lower intensity for a few days. If you are unsure, or have chest pain, shortness of breath, or lingering symptoms, check with a doctor.
