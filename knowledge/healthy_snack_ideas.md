# Healthy snack ideas
Category: meal

Snacks can bridge the gap between meals. Good options combine fibre or protein so they keep you satisfied: an apple or banana with peanut or almond butter, yogurt with berries, carrot and cucumber sticks with hummus, roasted chickpeas, a boiled egg, a handful of nuts, whole-grain crackers with cheese, or edamame.

Indian-style snacks include roasted makhana (fox nuts), sprouts chaat, fruit chaat, roasted chana, and buttermilk. For a quick energy snack before exercise, try a banana, dates with nuts, or toast with a thin spread of nut butter.

Keep simple snacks where you can see them, and choose ones you enjoy. If you have a nut allergy, swap in seeds or seed butters.
