# Indian meal ideas
Category: meal

Breakfast: idli or dosa with sambar and chutney, poha with peanuts and vegetables, vegetable upma, paneer or egg bhurji with roti, moong dal chilla, or oats with fruit.

Lunch and dinner: dal with rice or roti and a vegetable sabzi, rajma or chole with rice and a salad, vegetable khichdi with curd, chicken or egg curry with roti, tandoori chicken or paneer tikka with salad, or fish curry with rice.

Biryani and pulao are complete one-dish meals with rice and protein. Pairing them with raita and a side of salad or vegetables adds freshness and fibre. Curd, dal, and sprouts are easy ways to add protein to everyday meals.

Snacks: roasted chana, fruit chaat, buttermilk, sprouts salad, a handful of nuts, or a small bowl of curd.
