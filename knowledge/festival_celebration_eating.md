# Eating during festivals and celebrations
Category: general
Keywords: festival, diwali, holidays, celebration, party, sweets, enjoy food, balance
Summary: You can enjoy celebration foods and still feel good.

Celebrations are for enjoying food and family. Eat regular meals during the day, start with some protein and vegetables, savor your favorite treats slowly, and drink water. Stay active with a family walk or dancing.

No single meal or day undoes your progress. If you overate, simply return to your usual routine at the next meal without guilt or punishment.
