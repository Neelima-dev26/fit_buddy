# Lunch ideas collection
Category: meal
Keywords: lunch, packed lunch, lunch box, salad, wrap, bowl, office lunch
Summary: Balanced lunches combine protein, vegetables, and a whole grain.

Packable ideas: a chickpea salad with whole-grain bread, rice bowls with beans or tofu and roasted vegetables, a whole-wheat wrap with hummus and vegetables, dal with brown rice and a side salad, khichdi with curd, or pasta salad with beans and vegetables.

Use leftovers from dinner, keep a few dressings or chutneys handy, and add fruit or a small handful of nuts. Keep food cool in an insulated bag if it will sit for hours.
