# Full-body stretching routine
Category: wellness, workout

Do static stretches after a workout or when your muscles are warm. Hold each stretch for 20 to 30 seconds, breathe slowly, and do not bounce.

A simple sequence: calf stretch against a wall, standing quadriceps stretch, seated or standing hamstring stretch, kneeling hip flexor stretch, doorway chest stretch, cross-body shoulder stretch, overhead triceps stretch, and a gentle neck tilt to each side.

Stretch each side evenly and stop at mild tension, not pain. Stretching a few times a week supports flexibility, and it can be a pleasant way to wind down in the evening.
