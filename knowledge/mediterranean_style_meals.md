# Mediterranean-style meals
Category: meal

This style of eating centers on vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, fish, olive oil, nuts, and yogurt, with smaller amounts of meat and sweets.

Meal ideas: a Greek salad with chickpeas and whole-grain bread, grilled fish with roasted vegetables and rice, lentil soup with a side salad, a whole-grain pita with hummus and vegetables, pasta with tomatoes, vegetables, and beans, or yogurt with fruit and nuts for breakfast.

Cook with olive oil, flavor food with herbs, lemon, and garlic, and enjoy meals slowly, ideally with other people.
