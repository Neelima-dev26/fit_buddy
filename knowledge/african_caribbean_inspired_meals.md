# African and Caribbean inspired meal ideas
Category: meal
Keywords: african, caribbean, jollof, plantain, peanut stew, ethiopian, lentils, beans and rice
Summary: These cuisines offer hearty bean, grain, and vegetable dishes.

Ideas: Ethiopian lentil and vegetable stews with injera, West African peanut or vegetable stew with brown rice, jollof rice with grilled chicken or beans and a salad, Caribbean rice and peas with grilled fish and vegetables, or baked plantains with beans.

Many dishes combine legumes and grains for protein. Use less oil and salt when cooking, and add leafy greens and tomatoes to boost vegetables.
