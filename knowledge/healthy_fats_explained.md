# Healthy fats explained
Category: general
Keywords: fats, olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado, omega-3, fish, oils
Summary: Choosing mostly unsaturated fats supports heart health.

Unsaturated fats are found in olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado, and fatty fish such as salmon, sardines, and mackerel. Plant sources of omega-3 include walnuts, chia, and flax seeds.

Cooking with a small amount of oil is fine. Limit foods high in saturated fat and avoid trans fats. Because fats are energy dense, a small handful of nuts or a drizzle of oil is usually enough.
