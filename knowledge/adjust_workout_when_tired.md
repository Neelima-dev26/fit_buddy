# How to adjust your workout when you are tired
Category: workout, wellness
Keywords: tired, low energy, easy workout, fatigue, lighter session, adjust
Summary: On low-energy days, a lighter session is better than skipping or forcing a hard one.

If you slept badly or feel drained, try cutting the plan to about two thirds: fewer sets, lighter weights, or a shorter walk. Keep the warm-up, move slowly, and finish when you feel better than you started rather than worse.

Good low-energy options are a 15 to 20 minute walk, gentle yoga, easy cycling, or a few mobility moves. Stop and rest if you feel dizzy or unwell. One easy day does not undo your progress, and listening to your body helps you stay consistent.
