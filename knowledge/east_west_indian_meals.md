# East and West Indian meal ideas
Category: meal
Keywords: gujarati, maharashtrian, bengali, odia, thepla, dhokla, fish curry, poha, regional indian
Summary: Regional Indian cooking offers many balanced, plant-forward options.

West: Gujarati dhokla, thepla with curd, khichdi-kadhi, Maharashtrian poha, misal pav, bhakri with a vegetable, or sol kadhi. East: Bengali fish curry with rice, dal with vegetables, chana dal, or Odia dalma, which is dal cooked with vegetables.

Many of these meals combine lentils, vegetables, and grains, which makes them naturally balanced. Steamed or grilled options such as dhokla and fish are lighter choices.
