# How to do a squat: form guide
Category: form
Keywords: squat, bodyweight squat, goblet squat, legs, glutes, knees, form
Summary: The squat trains the legs and hips and is a good first strength move.

Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart and toes turned slightly out. Hold your chest up, brace your core, and sit your hips back and down as if lowering into a chair. Keep your knees tracking in line with your toes and your heels on the floor. Go as deep as you can with control, then press through your whole foot to stand.

Common fixes: if your heels lift, place a thin plate under them or widen your stance; if your knees cave in, think about pushing them out. A goblet squat, holding one dumbbell at your chest, helps you stay upright. Start with 2 sets of 8 to 12 slow repetitions.
