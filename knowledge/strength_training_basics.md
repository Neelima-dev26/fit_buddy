# Strength training basics
Category: general

To get stronger, challenge your muscles a little more over time, which is called progressive overload. You can add weight, repetitions, or sets, or slow down the movement.

For beginners, 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions with 60 to 90 seconds of rest works well. Choose a weight where you could do about one or two more repetitions with good form at the end of each set. Train each major muscle group two or three times a week with at least one rest day between hard sessions.

Technique comes first. Learn the movement with light weights, and consider asking a qualified trainer to check your form.
