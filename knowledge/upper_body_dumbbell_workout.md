# Upper-body dumbbell workout
Category: workout

A simple upper-body session needs only a pair of dumbbells. Warm up for 5 minutes with arm circles and light shoulder movements.

Do 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions of each exercise, resting about 60 seconds between sets: dumbbell shoulder press, one-arm dumbbell row, dumbbell chest press (lying on the floor or a bench), biceps curl, and overhead triceps extension.

Pick a weight that makes the last two repetitions challenging while your form stays good. Move slowly on the way down, keep your core tight, and avoid swinging the weights. Train upper body two or three days per week, with a rest day between sessions.
