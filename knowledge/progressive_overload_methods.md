# Progressive overload: ways to make exercise harder
Category: general
Keywords: progressive overload, progress, harder, increase, plateau, variation
Summary: You can progress by changing one variable at a time.

Ways to progress: add repetitions, add a set, use a slightly heavier weight, slow down the lowering phase, shorten rest periods, or move to a harder variation, such as from knee to full push-ups.

Change only one thing at a time and give your body one or two weeks to adjust. If form breaks down, go back a step. Progress does not have to be every week, and recovery weeks are part of the process.
