# High-intensity interval training for beginners
Category: workout

Interval training alternates short bursts of harder effort with rest. A beginner session could be 20 to 30 seconds of effort followed by 40 to 60 seconds of easy movement, repeated 6 to 8 times, after a 5-minute warm-up.

Choose moves that suit you: fast marching, brisk stationary cycling, step jacks, bodyweight squats, or mountain climbers on an incline. Low-impact choices work well for beginners and for sensitive joints.

Do interval sessions only one or two days per week, with rest days between, and finish with a 5-minute cool-down. Effort should feel hard but controlled. Stop if you feel dizzy, unwell, or have chest discomfort, and check with a doctor first if you have health concerns.
