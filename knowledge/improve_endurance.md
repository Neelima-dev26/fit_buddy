# Improving endurance
Category: workout
Keywords: endurance, stamina, aerobic, cardio, running, cycling, build up
Summary: Build endurance gradually with regular, moderate cardio.

Do 3 to 5 sessions a week of walking, jogging, cycling, or swimming at a conversational pace, and increase total time by about 5 to 10 minutes per week. Add one longer session on the weekend and an occasional interval session, such as 1 minute faster and 2 minutes easy.

Take an easier week every third or fourth week. Fuel up, drink water, and sleep well. Strength training twice a week supports endurance by improving efficiency and lowering injury risk.
