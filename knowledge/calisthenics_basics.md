# Calisthenics basics
Category: workout
Keywords: calisthenics, bodyweight training, pull-up, dips, push-up, progression
Summary: Calisthenics builds strength using your own body weight.

Common calisthenics moves are push-ups, squats, lunges, planks, dips, pull-ups, and leg raises. Each has easier and harder versions, so you can progress without a gym.

A simple routine: 3 rounds of push-ups, squats, rows or pull-ups, lunges, and a plank, three times a week. Increase difficulty by slowing the tempo, adding pauses, or switching to one-arm or one-leg versions over time.
