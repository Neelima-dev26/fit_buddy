# Quick dinner ideas in 30 minutes
Category: meal
Keywords: dinner, quick dinner, 30 minutes, easy recipes, weeknight, one pan
Summary: Simple dinners can be ready in half an hour.

Try a vegetable and tofu or chicken stir-fry with rice, egg fried rice with frozen vegetables, dal and rice with a quick vegetable sabzi, a sheet-pan meal of fish or paneer with vegetables, bean and vegetable quesadillas, or pasta with a tomato and vegetable sauce.

Prep ingredients ahead, cook grains in batches, and keep frozen vegetables, canned beans, and eggs on hand. Season with herbs, spices, and lemon for flavor.
