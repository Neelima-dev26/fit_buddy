# Yoga and mobility basics
Category: wellness, workout

Gentle yoga and mobility work can improve flexibility and help you relax. Move slowly and breathe steadily through each pose.

A 10-minute beginner flow: cat-cow (8 slow rounds), child's pose (30 seconds), downward-facing dog (3 slow breaths), low lunge on each side (30 seconds), cobra or sphinx pose (3 breaths), seated forward fold with bent knees (30 seconds), and a final lying twist on each side.

Stretch to mild tension only and never push into pain. A folded blanket or cushion can make poses more comfortable. Practicing a few minutes most days is better than one long session. This is a great option for rest days.
