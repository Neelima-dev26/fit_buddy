# Beginner resistance band plan
Category: workout

Resistance bands are inexpensive, light, and easy to use at home. Train three days per week with rest days between. Start with a 5-minute warm-up.

Do 2 to 3 sets of 10 to 15 repetitions of each exercise: banded squats (band above the knees), banded rows (anchor the band or loop it around your feet), band pull-aparts for the upper back, standing banded chest press, and glute bridges with a band above the knees. Move slowly and control the band on the way back, rather than letting it snap.

Use a lighter band until your form is smooth, then move to a stronger band or add repetitions. Check the band for tears before each use.
