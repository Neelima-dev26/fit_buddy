# Biceps and triceps exercises
Category: form
Keywords: biceps curl, triceps extension, triceps dip, arms
Summary: Simple arm exercises round out an upper-body routine.

Biceps curl: hold a dumbbell in each hand, elbows near your sides, and curl the weights toward your shoulders, then lower slowly without swinging. Triceps extension: hold one dumbbell with both hands overhead, bend your elbows to lower it behind your head, and press back up. Bench dips and close-grip push-ups also work the triceps.

Your arms already work during rows and presses, so one or two arm exercises at the end of a session are plenty. Use 2 sets of 10 to 15 repetitions with a lighter weight and good control.
