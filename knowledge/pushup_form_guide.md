# How to do a push-up: form guide and easier versions
Category: form
Keywords: push-up, pushup, chest, triceps, shoulders, incline, knee push-up, form
Summary: A push-up works the chest, shoulders, triceps, and core, and can be scaled to any level.

Place your hands slightly wider than your shoulders and make a straight line from head to heels. Lower your chest toward the floor with your elbows at about a 45-degree angle from your body, then press back up without letting your hips sag.

Make it easier by raising your hands on a wall, counter, or sturdy bench, or by doing knee push-ups. Make it harder by slowing the lowering phase or elevating your feet. Aim for 2 to 3 sets of as many good repetitions as you can, and progress when you can do 3 sets of 10 with good form.
