# North Indian meal ideas
Category: meal
Keywords: north indian, roti, dal, paneer, rajma, chole, sabzi, paratha, curd
Summary: North Indian meals usually pair roti or rice with dal, vegetables, and curd.

Examples: roti with dal and a seasonal sabzi, rajma or chole with rice and salad, paneer bhurji with roti, vegetable pulao with raita, stuffed paratha with curd, or tandoori chicken or fish with salad.

Choose whole-wheat or multigrain rotis, add extra vegetables to curries, and balance richer dishes with salad and curd. Baking, grilling, and pressure cooking can lighten dishes.
