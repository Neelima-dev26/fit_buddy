# A healthy relationship with food
Category: wellness, general
Keywords: food guilt, good and bad foods, restriction, binge, emotional eating, eating disorder
Summary: No food is good or bad, and balance beats strict rules.

Healthy eating includes foods you enjoy, regular meals, and flexibility. Labeling foods as good or bad can create guilt and cycles of restricting and overeating. Aim for variety and balance over time.

If you often skip meals, feel anxious about food, binge, purge, or feel out of control around eating, please talk to a doctor or mental health professional. In the US, the National Alliance for Eating Disorders offers support at allianceforeatingdisorders.com.
