# Mexican and Latin inspired meals
Category: meal

These cuisines offer filling meals built on beans, corn, vegetables, and lean proteins.

Ideas: a burrito bowl with rice, black beans, salsa, and vegetables, chicken or bean tacos on corn tortillas topped with shredded cabbage, vegetable fajitas, black bean soup, eggs with beans and tortillas for breakfast, grilled corn salad, or guacamole with vegetable sticks.

Beans and rice together make a satisfying meal with plenty of fibre. Fresh salsa, lime, cilantro, and spices add flavor without much effort.
