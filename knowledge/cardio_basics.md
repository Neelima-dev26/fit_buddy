# Cardio basics for beginners
Category: workout

Cardio, or aerobic exercise, raises your heart rate and builds endurance. Good beginner options are brisk walking, easy cycling, swimming, and dancing.

Use the talk test to check your effort. At a moderate effort you can talk but not sing. At a vigorous effort you can say only a few words at a time. Most beginner cardio should feel moderate.

A simple start is a 20 to 30 minute brisk walk three times per week. Walk-run intervals can be a gentle way to start running: alternate 1 minute of easy jogging with 2 minutes of walking, repeated 6 to 8 times, and slowly increase the jogging time over several weeks. Wear comfortable, supportive shoes.
