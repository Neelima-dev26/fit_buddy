# Beginner bodyweight plan (no equipment)
Category: workout

A simple full-body plan for three non-consecutive days per week, such as Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Start with a 5-minute warm-up.

Do each exercise for 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions, resting 45 to 60 seconds between sets: bodyweight squats, incline or knee push-ups, glute bridges, reverse lunges (alternating legs), and a plank held for 15 to 30 seconds. Finish with a 5-minute cool-down.

For a shorter session of 15 to 20 minutes, do 2 sets of each exercise. As it gets easier, add repetitions first, then add a third set, then try a harder variation such as full push-ups.

On the other days, an easy walk or light stretching is a good addition.
