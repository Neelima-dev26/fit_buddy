# Fitness terms glossary
Category: general
Keywords: glossary, terms, DOMS, HIIT, PR, RPE, superset, circuit, warm-up, cooldown
Summary: A quick guide to common gym words.

DOMS is delayed onset muscle soreness, the ache a day or two after a new workout. HIIT is high-intensity interval training. A circuit is a series of exercises done one after another. A superset pairs two exercises back to back. RPE is rate of perceived exertion, a 1 to 10 scale for effort. A PR is a personal record. Cardio is aerobic exercise that raises your heart rate.

Compound exercises, such as squats and rows, use several joints and muscle groups, while isolation exercises, such as curls, focus on one.
