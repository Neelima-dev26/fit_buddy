# Stress relief through movement
Category: wellness, workout
Keywords: stress, anxiety, mood, walking, yoga, exercise for stress, calm
Summary: Gentle, regular movement can lift your mood and ease stress.

A 10 to 20 minute walk, preferably outdoors, is one of the simplest stress relievers. Yoga, stretching, swimming, cycling, and dancing also help. Rhythmic, enjoyable activities work best, and you do not need to push hard.

Combine movement with slow breathing or music. Notice how you feel afterward, which can help you stay motivated. If stress or low mood is constant or overwhelming, talk to a doctor or mental health professional.
