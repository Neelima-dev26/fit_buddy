# Eating with food allergies
Category: general

Common food allergens include milk, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, fish, shellfish, soy, wheat, and sesame. Allergic reactions can be serious.

If you have a diagnosed allergy, follow your doctor's plan and read the ingredient list every time you buy or eat packaged food, since recipes change. Be careful about cross-contact in kitchens, shared utensils, and restaurants, and tell staff about your allergy when eating out.

FitBuddy cannot assess allergies or medical dietary needs. If you are unsure whether you have an allergy, see a doctor or allergist. For emergencies such as trouble breathing or swelling, call your local emergency number right away.
