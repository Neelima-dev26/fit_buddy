# Five-minute desk break routine
Category: wellness, workout

If you sit for long periods, a short break every hour helps. Stand up and walk around for a minute or two.

A simple routine with no equipment: shoulder rolls (10), slow neck tilts side to side (5 each side), a seated or standing spinal twist (20 seconds each side), standing hip flexor stretch (20 seconds each side), calf raises (15), and 10 slow bodyweight squats. Finish with three slow deep breaths.

Keep every movement gentle, and stop if anything hurts. This is a good habit to add to your daily checklist.
