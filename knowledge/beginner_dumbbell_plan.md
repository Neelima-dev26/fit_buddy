# Beginner dumbbell plan
Category: workout

A full-body plan for three days per week with at least one rest day between sessions. Begin with a 5-minute warm-up.

Do 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions of each exercise, resting about 60 seconds between sets: goblet squats (hold one dumbbell at your chest), one-arm dumbbell rows, dumbbell shoulder press, dumbbell Romanian deadlifts (hinge at the hips with a flat back), and a plank for 20 to 30 seconds. Finish with a 5-minute cool-down.

Choose a weight that feels challenging by the last two repetitions while your form stays good. When you can complete all sets with good form, increase the weight slightly or add a repetition or two. For a 30-minute session, aim for 3 sets of 4 or 5 exercises.
