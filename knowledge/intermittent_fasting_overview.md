# Intermittent fasting: an overview
Category: general
Keywords: intermittent fasting, 16:8, time restricted eating, fasting window, diet trend
Summary: Intermittent fasting is an eating pattern, not a requirement, and it is not right for everyone.

Intermittent fasting limits eating to certain hours or days, for example eating within an 8 to 10 hour window. Research on its benefits compared with other healthy eating patterns is mixed, and what matters most is food quality and what you can sustain.

It is not recommended for children and teenagers, pregnant or breastfeeding people, people with diabetes or on medication, or anyone with a history of disordered eating, unless a doctor advises it. Talk to a doctor first.
