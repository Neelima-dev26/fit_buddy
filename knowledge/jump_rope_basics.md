# Jump rope basics
Category: workout
Keywords: jump rope, skipping, cardio, coordination, low equipment
Summary: Jump rope is an inexpensive cardio workout.

Choose a rope that reaches your armpits when you stand on the middle. Jump low, land softly on the balls of your feet, and turn the rope from your wrists. Start with 20 to 30 seconds of jumping, then rest for 30 to 60 seconds, repeated for 5 to 10 minutes.

It is high-impact, so use a forgiving surface and supportive shoes, and skip it if you have knee or ankle pain. Marching or step jacks without a rope are lower-impact options.
