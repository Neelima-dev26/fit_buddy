# Simple cooking methods
Category: general

How you cook can change a meal. Steaming, boiling, baking, grilling, roasting, pressure cooking, and sauteing with a small amount of oil all work well and keep food flavorful.

Roast vegetables with a little oil and spices until golden. Pressure cook or simmer lentils and beans for easy protein. Bake or grill fish and chicken instead of deep frying when you can, and save fried foods for occasional treats.

Season with herbs, spices, garlic, lemon, and vinegar for flavor. Wash hands and surfaces, and cook meat, poultry, and eggs thoroughly for food safety.
