# Caffeine and alcohol: an overview
Category: general, wellness
Keywords: caffeine, coffee, tea, energy drinks, alcohol, sleep, hydration
Summary: Moderate caffeine is fine for many adults, and alcohol affects sleep and recovery.

Coffee and tea can be part of a healthy day for most adults. Avoid caffeine late in the day if it affects your sleep, and be cautious with energy drinks, especially for teenagers. Pregnant people and those with heart conditions should check with their doctor.

Alcohol can disrupt sleep and slow recovery, and it is best avoided or kept moderate by adults who choose to drink. It is not recommended for people under legal age, pregnant people, or those with certain conditions or medications.
