# Foam rolling and active recovery
Category: general, wellness
Keywords: foam roller, recovery, massage, mobility, rest day, light movement
Summary: Gentle movement and optional foam rolling can help you feel better between sessions.

Active recovery means low-effort movement on rest days, such as walking, easy cycling, swimming, or gentle yoga. It helps you feel less stiff.

Foam rolling can feel good for sore muscles: roll slowly over a muscle for 30 to 60 seconds, pausing on tight spots, and avoid rolling directly over joints or bones. It should feel like a firm massage, not sharp pain. Good sleep and enough food and water matter most for recovery.
