# Pull-ups and lat pulldowns: how to progress
Category: form
Keywords: pull-up, chin-up, lat pulldown, assisted pull-up, back, lats
Summary: Pulling from above builds the back and arms, and it can be scaled with machines or bands.

If you cannot do a pull-up yet, start with a lat pulldown machine, a band-assisted pull-up, or slow negatives, where you jump or step to the top and lower yourself over 3 to 5 seconds. Hang with your shoulders pulled down away from your ears, and pull your chest toward the bar.

Do 2 to 3 sets of 5 to 10 repetitions, and add a repetition each week. Rows and band pull-aparts help build the strength you need. Avoid swinging your body, and stop if you feel shoulder pain.
