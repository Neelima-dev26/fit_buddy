# Exercising in hot and cold weather
Category: workout

In hot weather, exercise earlier or later in the day, wear light breathable clothing, drink water before, during, and after, and slow down. Stop and cool off if you feel dizzy, nauseated, have a headache, or feel confused, and seek medical help if the symptoms do not improve.

In cold weather, wear layers you can remove, protect your hands, ears, and head, and warm up a little longer. Cold air can feel harsh when breathing hard, so a scarf over your mouth can help.

Indoor options such as walking in a mall, a home workout, or a gym are good backups on extreme weather days.
