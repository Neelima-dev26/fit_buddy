# Fibre and gut health
Category: meal, general

Fibre supports digestion and helps you feel full. Good sources include vegetables, fruit with the skin, whole grains such as oats and brown rice, beans, lentils, nuts, and seeds.

Increase fibre gradually and drink enough water, since a sudden increase can cause gas or bloating. Easy swaps: choose whole-grain bread or rice, add beans to soups and salads, include a vegetable at every meal, and snack on fruit.

Fermented foods such as yogurt, kefir, buttermilk, kimchi, and sauerkraut may also be part of a varied diet. If you have ongoing digestive problems, speak to a doctor.
