# Prep-ahead breakfasts and snacks
Category: meal
Keywords: meal prep, breakfast, overnight oats, egg muffins, snack boxes, smoothie packs, grab and go
Summary: Breakfasts and snacks take only minutes to prepare in bulk.

Make five jars of overnight oats, a tray of egg muffins with vegetables, or a batch of oat and banana pancakes to reheat. Freeze smoothie packs of fruit, spinach, and banana in bags. Pre-portion nuts, roasted chickpeas, or makhana, and slice vegetables for dips.

Keep fruit visible, prepare yogurt cups with berries, and boil a few eggs. These grab-and-go options make busy mornings easier and help you choose snacks that fit your plan.
