# Meal prep plan: vegetarian Indian week
Category: meal
Keywords: meal prep, vegetarian, indian, dal, rice, sabzi, batch, sunday
Summary: A sample prep session for a vegetarian Indian week.

Cook a large pot of dal (toor, moong, or masoor), a pot of rice or a millet such as ragi or jowar, and two sabzis such as mixed vegetables and cabbage. Boil chickpeas or rajma, and make a jar of chutney or raita base. Chop onion, tomato, and green chili for quick tadkas.

Through the week: dal and rice with sabzi and curd, chana masala with roti, khichdi with curd, and sprouts chaat as a snack. Freeze extra dal and curry in single portions, and cook fresh roti on the day.
