# Carbohydrates explained
Category: general
Keywords: carbs, whole grains, sugar, fibre, rice, bread, pasta, energy
Summary: Carbs are not the enemy, and whole-food sources provide fibre and steady energy.

Good carbohydrate sources include oats, brown rice, whole-wheat roti and bread, quinoa, beans, lentils, fruit, and starchy vegetables like potatoes and sweet potatoes. Pair carbs with protein and vegetables for steady energy.

Refined foods such as sweets and sugary drinks digest quickly and are best enjoyed in moderation. Before a workout, a small carb snack like a banana or toast can help, and after a workout carbs help you recover.
