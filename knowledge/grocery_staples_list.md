# A basic grocery staples list
Category: meal

A well-stocked kitchen makes simple meals easier.

Fresh: seasonal vegetables and fruit, leafy greens, onions, garlic, and ginger. Grains: oats, rice, whole-wheat flour or bread, quinoa, and pasta. Proteins: eggs, lentils and dal, canned or dried beans and chickpeas, tofu, chicken, fish, and paneer or yogurt. Frozen: mixed vegetables and berries. Pantry: canned tomatoes, nuts and seeds, olive or other cooking oil, spices, and herbs.

Plan a few meals before shopping, write a list, and check what you already have. Frozen and canned vegetables and beans are nutritious, convenient, and often cheaper.
