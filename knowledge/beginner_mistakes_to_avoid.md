# Common beginner mistakes
Category: general
Keywords: mistakes, beginner, too much too soon, skipping warm-up, form, rest
Summary: Most beginner problems come from doing too much too soon.

Common mistakes include skipping the warm-up, lifting too heavy with poor form, training the same muscles every day, ignoring rest and sleep, comparing yourself to others, and expecting fast results. Another is following an extreme plan that is hard to maintain.

Start light, learn the movements, add small amounts each week, and choose a schedule you can keep. Celebrate consistency and listen to your body.
