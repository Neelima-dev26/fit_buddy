# Weekly exercise guidelines for adults
Category: general

Most health agencies suggest that adults aim for 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity each week, such as brisk walking, easy cycling, or swimming. Alternatively, 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous activity, such as running, can be used. Any amount of movement is better than none, so beginners can start small and build up.

Adults are also advised to do muscle-strengthening activities on two or more days each week, working all the major muscle groups: legs, hips, back, chest, shoulders, and arms. Bodyweight exercises, dumbbells, and resistance bands all count.

Increase time and effort gradually. A good approach is to add a few minutes to a session or one extra session per week, rather than doing too much too soon.

Reference: U.S. Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans (2nd edition) and WHO guidelines on physical activity (2020).
