# Workouts while traveling or busy
Category: workout

You can stay active anywhere. A hotel room routine needs no equipment: bodyweight squats, push-ups against a bed or desk, lunges, a plank, and glute bridges, done as a 15 to 20 minute circuit.

Pack a resistance band, which is light and gives you rows, presses, and leg work. Walk to explore a new place, take the stairs, and stretch after long flights or drives.

On very busy days, aim for two or three 10-minute sessions of movement instead of skipping. Short sessions still count toward your weekly activity.
