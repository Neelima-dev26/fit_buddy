# Eating out and takeaway tips
Category: general
Keywords: restaurant, takeaway, eating out, ordering, fast food, menu choices
Summary: You can enjoy restaurant meals with a few easy choices.

Look for dishes with vegetables, a protein, and a whole grain or starch, such as grilled or baked mains with sides, dal or bean dishes, or salads with protein. Share starters or desserts, ask for sauces on the side, and choose water as your main drink.

Eat slowly and stop when you are comfortably full. Enjoy treats without guilt. Tell the staff about allergies and check ingredients, especially for dishes with sauces.
