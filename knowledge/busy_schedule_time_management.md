# Fitting fitness into a busy schedule
Category: wellness, workout
Keywords: busy, time, schedule, planning, short workouts, priorities, calendar
Summary: Small blocks and a plan make it easier to be active.

Look at your week and pick two or three realistic slots, even 10 to 20 minutes, and put them in your calendar. Combine activity with other tasks: walk during phone calls, take stairs, or cycle for errands.

Have a backup plan for busy days, such as a 10-minute circuit. Done is better than perfect, and consistency over weeks matters more than any single workout.
