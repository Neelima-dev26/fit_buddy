# Naps and tiredness
Category: wellness

A short nap of 10 to 20 minutes, ideally before mid-afternoon, can boost alertness. Longer or later naps may make it harder to sleep at night.

If you feel tired during the day, check the basics: enough sleep, regular meals, water, daylight, and some movement. A short walk often helps more than another coffee.

Ongoing exhaustion, very heavy sleepiness, or tiredness that does not improve with good sleep can have medical causes, so talk to a doctor if it continues.
