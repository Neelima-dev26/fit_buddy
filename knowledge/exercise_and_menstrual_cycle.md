# Exercise and the menstrual cycle
Category: general
Keywords: period, menstrual cycle, cramps, energy, hormones, women
Summary: Energy can change across the cycle, and light movement often helps with cramps.

Some people feel less energetic around their period, while others feel fine. Gentle walking, yoga, and stretching may ease cramps, and you can lower the intensity if you feel tired.

Track how you feel across the month and plan harder sessions for higher-energy days. Stay hydrated and eat regular meals. If you have severe pain, very heavy bleeding, or missed periods, talk to a doctor.
