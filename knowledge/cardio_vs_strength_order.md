# Cardio or strength first?
Category: general
Keywords: cardio first, strength first, order, same day, combine
Summary: Do the part that matters most to you first, when you are freshest.

If your main goal is strength, lift first and finish with easy cardio. If your goal is endurance, do cardio first. For general fitness, either order works, and you can also do them on separate days.

Keep the second part moderate so you do not get too tired to use good form. A short warm-up before both is important, and on days when you do both, keep the total time realistic.
