# Fitness basics for teenagers
Category: general, wellness

Children and teenagers aged 6 to 17 are generally advised to get at least 60 minutes of moderate to vigorous activity every day. That can include sports, walking, cycling, dancing, and active play. Muscle-strengthening and bone-strengthening activities, such as bodyweight exercises, jumping, and running, are recommended on at least three days a week.

Focus on fun, variety, and good technique. Heavy lifting should be supervised by a qualified coach or adult. Teens need around 8 to 10 hours of sleep and balanced meals to support growth.

Avoid dieting or comparing yourself with others online. If you have questions about your body, a parent, doctor, or school nurse can help.
