# Middle Eastern meals
Category: meal

Middle Eastern cooking features legumes, vegetables, whole grains, yogurt, and herbs.

Ideas: hummus with whole-wheat pita and a salad, a falafel wrap with vegetables, shakshuka (eggs poached in spiced tomato sauce), lentil soup, tabbouleh with parsley and bulgur, grilled chicken or vegetable kebabs with rice and salad, or yogurt with cucumber and mint.

Many of these dishes are naturally vegetarian. Baking or grilling instead of frying is a lighter option, and a squeeze of lemon brightens almost any dish.
