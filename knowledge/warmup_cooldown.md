# Warm-up and cool-down
Category: workout

A warm-up of 5 to 10 minutes prepares your muscles and joints. Start with easy movement such as marching in place, brisk walking, or gentle jumping jacks, then add dynamic moves like arm circles, leg swings, hip circles, bodyweight squats, and walking lunges. Keep the effort light and the movements controlled.

A cool-down of 5 minutes helps your heart rate come down gradually. Walk slowly, then do gentle static stretches, holding each for 20 to 30 seconds without bouncing. Typical stretches target the calves, hamstrings, quadriceps, hips, chest, and shoulders. Stretch only to mild tension, never to sharp pain.
