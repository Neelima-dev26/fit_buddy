# Batch cooking guide for the week
Category: meal, general
Keywords: meal prep, batch cooking, sunday prep, cook once eat twice, prep day, leftovers
Summary: Cook a few building blocks once and mix them into different meals all week.

Pick one prep session a week, often Sunday, and cook building blocks instead of full meals: a pot of rice or quinoa, a big pot of dal, beans, or lentils, a tray of roasted vegetables, and a protein such as boiled eggs, baked chicken or fish, tofu, or paneer. Wash and chop raw vegetables and fruit.

During the week, mix and match: rice with dal and salad, a grain bowl with beans and roasted vegetables, or a wrap with leftover protein. Prep takes about 2 to 3 hours, and cooked dishes keep in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days.
