# How hard should I work? Effort scale and talk test
Category: general
Keywords: RPE, effort, intensity, talk test, moderate, vigorous
Summary: Use the talk test or a 1 to 10 effort scale to pace yourself.

On a scale of 1 to 10, aim for about 5 to 6 for moderate exercise and 7 to 8 for vigorous exercise. With the talk test, you can talk but not sing during moderate activity, and you can say only a few words at a time during vigorous activity.

For strength training, finish each set feeling you could do one or two more good reps. Beginners should mostly stay at moderate effort. Stop if you feel dizzy, faint, or have chest pain.
