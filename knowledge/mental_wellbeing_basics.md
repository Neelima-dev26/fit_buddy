# Mental wellbeing basics
Category: wellness

Mental health is as important as physical health. Helpful habits include regular movement, enough sleep, time outdoors, staying connected with people you trust, and taking breaks from news and social media.

Everyone has hard days. If low mood, worry, or stress lasts for more than a couple of weeks, gets in the way of daily life, or makes you feel hopeless, please speak with a doctor or mental health professional.

If you ever feel you might harm yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line right away. In the US you can call or text 988. You do not have to deal with this alone.
