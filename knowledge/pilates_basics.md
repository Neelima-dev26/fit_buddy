# Pilates basics
Category: workout, wellness
Keywords: pilates, core, mat pilates, hundred, bridge, posture
Summary: Pilates uses slow, controlled movements to build core strength and posture.

Mat Pilates needs only a mat. Beginner moves include the hundred (small arm pumps with legs raised or bent), pelvic curls, single-leg stretch, side-lying leg lifts, and spine twists. Move slowly, breathe steadily, and keep your core engaged.

Try 15 to 20 minutes two or three times a week, or follow a beginner class. It pairs well with strength training and walking. Modify any move that strains your neck or back.
