# Core strength basics
Category: workout

Your core includes the muscles of the abdomen, back, and hips. A strong core helps posture and supports most other exercises.

Good beginner moves are the plank (hold 15 to 30 seconds), dead bug (lie on your back and lower opposite arm and leg slowly), bird dog (on hands and knees, reach opposite arm and leg), glute bridge, and side plank on the knees. Do 2 to 3 rounds, two or three times a week.

Breathe steadily and keep your lower back comfortable. If a move strains your neck or back, shorten the hold or choose an easier version. Quality matters more than time. Stop a hold when your form breaks.
