# Focus and breaks for study and work
Category: wellness
Keywords: focus, study, concentration, pomodoro, breaks, productivity, procrastination
Summary: Short breaks help you concentrate better.

Try working for 25 to 50 minutes, then take a 5 to 10 minute break to stand, stretch, and drink water. Keep your phone away during work blocks, and tackle the hardest task when your energy is highest.

Short bouts of movement can boost alertness. Get enough sleep and eat regular meals to support focus, and take a longer break at lunch.
