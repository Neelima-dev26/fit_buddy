# Supplements: what to know
Category: general
Keywords: supplements, protein powder, creatine, multivitamin, caffeine pills, safety
Summary: Supplements are optional and not a replacement for food, and a doctor can advise you.

Most people do not need supplements if they eat a varied diet. Products like protein powder and creatine are common among lifters, but they are not required for beginners. Some supplements can interact with medicines or are unsuitable for teenagers, pregnancy, or certain conditions.

Talk to a doctor or dietitian before starting any supplement, choose products tested by a third party, and avoid anything promising fast results. FitBuddy does not recommend specific supplements.
