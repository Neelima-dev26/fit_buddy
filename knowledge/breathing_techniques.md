# Breathing techniques for stress
Category: wellness

Slow breathing can help you feel calmer within a minute or two. Sit or lie comfortably and place a hand on your belly.

Extended exhale: breathe in through your nose for about 4 seconds and out slowly through your mouth for about 6 seconds. Box breathing: breathe in for 4, hold for 4, breathe out for 4, and hold for 4. Repeat for one to three minutes.

If you feel lightheaded, return to normal breathing. Try it before bed, before a stressful meeting, or after a workout. If stress or anxiety feels constant or overwhelming, please talk to a doctor or mental health professional.
