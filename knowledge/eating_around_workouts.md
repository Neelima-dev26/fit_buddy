# Eating around workouts
Category: meal

You do not need special foods for a beginner workout. If you feel hungry, a small snack one to two hours before, such as a banana, toast with peanut butter, or yogurt, can give you energy. Avoid a very large meal right before exercise, since it can feel uncomfortable.

After a workout, a meal or snack with protein and carbohydrates, such as eggs and toast, yogurt with fruit, or rice and dal, helps you recover. Drink water before, during, and after exercise.

Listen to your hunger. If you feel dizzy, weak, or unwell during exercise, stop and rest, and speak to a doctor if it keeps happening.
