# Lunges and split squats: form guide
Category: form
Keywords: lunge, reverse lunge, split squat, step-up, legs, balance, knees
Summary: Lunges train one leg at a time, building strength and balance.

For a reverse lunge, stand tall and step one foot back, lowering until both knees are bent at about 90 degrees. Keep your front knee over your ankle, your torso upright, and push through the front foot to return. Alternate legs.

If balance is a challenge, hold a wall or chair, or try a split squat, where your feet stay in place and you simply lower and rise. Step-ups onto a low, sturdy step are another gentle option. Do 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 10 repetitions per leg. Stop if you feel sharp knee pain.
