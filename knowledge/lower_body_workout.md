# Lower-body workout
Category: workout

Strong legs and hips support everyday movement. Warm up for 5 minutes with marching in place, leg swings, and a few easy bodyweight squats.

Do 2 to 3 sets of 10 to 15 repetitions: bodyweight or goblet squats, reverse lunges, glute bridges, step-ups onto a sturdy step, and standing calf raises. Add a dumbbell once the movements feel easy.

Keep your knees pointing in the same direction as your toes, push through your whole foot, and move with control. Leave one or two days between lower-body sessions so your muscles can recover. Finish with gentle stretches for the calves, hamstrings, and hips.
