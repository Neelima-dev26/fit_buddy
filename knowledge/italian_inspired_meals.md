# Italian-inspired meal ideas
Category: meal
Keywords: italian, pasta, minestrone, caprese, whole wheat pasta, tomato sauce
Summary: Italian-style meals can be vegetable-rich and satisfying.

Ideas: whole-wheat pasta with a tomato and vegetable sauce and white beans, minestrone soup, a caprese salad with whole-grain bread, grilled fish with roasted vegetables, a vegetable frittata, or a thin-crust vegetable pizza with a side salad.

Use olive oil, garlic, herbs, and tomatoes for flavor, add beans or chicken for protein, and pair pasta with plenty of vegetables.
