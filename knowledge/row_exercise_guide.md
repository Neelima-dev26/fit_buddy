# Rows: form guide for back strength
Category: form
Keywords: row, dumbbell row, band row, seated row, back, lats, posture
Summary: Rows strengthen the upper back and help balance all the pushing you do.

For a one-arm dumbbell row, place one hand and knee on a bench or sturdy chair and hold a dumbbell in the other hand with your back flat. Pull the weight toward your hip, squeezing your shoulder blade toward your spine, then lower slowly.

You can also use a resistance band anchored to a door, or a seated row machine. Keep your neck relaxed and avoid twisting. Do 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions per side. Rows are a good counterbalance to desk work and push-ups.
