# Running for beginners
Category: workout

Start with walk-run intervals instead of running continuously. A sample six-week plan, three days per week: weeks 1 and 2, jog 1 minute and walk 2 minutes, repeated 8 times; weeks 3 and 4, jog 3 minutes and walk 1 to 2 minutes, repeated 5 or 6 times; weeks 5 and 6, jog 5 to 8 minutes with short walks, building toward 20 to 30 minutes.

Run at an easy pace where you can still talk. Wear supportive running shoes and warm up with a 5-minute brisk walk. Leave a rest day between runs.

If a week feels too hard, repeat it. Gradual progress lowers the chance of sore shins and tired joints. Stop and rest if you feel sharp pain.
