# Recovery and muscle soreness
Category: general, wellness

Muscle soreness that appears a day after a new workout and fades within two to three days is common, especially for beginners. Light movement such as walking or gentle stretching, plenty of water, and good sleep usually help you feel better.

Rest days are when your body adapts and gets stronger. Many people leave one to two days between strength sessions for the same muscle groups. If you feel unusually tired, an easy day or a rest day is a good choice.

Stop an exercise if you feel sharp pain, joint pain, dizziness, or anything that feels wrong, and see a healthcare professional if it continues. Soreness is different from pain.
