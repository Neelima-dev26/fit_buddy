# Choosing workout shoes and clothing
Category: general
Keywords: shoes, sneakers, running shoes, clothing, fit, comfortable, gear
Summary: Comfortable, supportive shoes and breathable clothes make exercise easier.

Choose shoes designed for your main activity: running shoes for running, flatter, stable shoes for weight training, and comfortable walking shoes for walking. Shoes should fit well with a thumb's width of space at the toe, and replace running shoes when they are worn out.

Wear breathable, moisture-wicking fabrics and layers you can remove. Good socks help prevent blisters, and a supportive sports bra is important for many people.
