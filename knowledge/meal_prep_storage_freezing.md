# Storing, freezing, and reheating prepped food
Category: meal, general
Keywords: food storage, freezing, reheating, leftovers, food safety, containers, fridge
Summary: Good storage keeps prepped food safe and tasty.

Cool cooked food quickly, put it in shallow covered containers, and refrigerate within 2 hours. Eat most dishes within 3 to 4 days. Freeze soups, curries, stews, cooked beans, and rice in single portions for up to a few months, labeled with the date.

Thaw in the refrigerator, and reheat until steaming hot throughout, stirring once. Do not reheat rice more than once, and do not leave cooked rice at room temperature for long. Keep raw meat and fish away from ready-to-eat foods.
