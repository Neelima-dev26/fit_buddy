# Walking plan for beginners
Category: workout

Walking is one of the easiest ways to start. Begin with a 10 to 15 minute brisk walk, three or four days per week. A brisk pace is one where you can talk but not sing.

Add about 5 minutes each week. A sample progression: week 1 is 15 minutes, week 2 is 20, week 3 is 25, and week 4 is 30 minutes, most days of the week. Later you can add short faster stretches, hills, or longer weekend walks.

Wear comfortable shoes, stand tall, and swing your arms naturally. Walk with a friend, a podcast, or music to make it enjoyable. Short 10-minute walks after meals also count toward your weekly activity.
