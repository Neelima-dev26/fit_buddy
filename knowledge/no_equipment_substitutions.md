# Exercise swaps when you have no equipment
Category: workout
Keywords: no equipment, bodyweight, substitute, swap, home, alternatives
Summary: Almost every gym exercise has a bodyweight or household swap.

Instead of a shoulder press, try pike push-ups; for rows, use a sturdy table (lie under it and pull up) or a backpack with books in a bent-over row. Replace goblet squats with bodyweight or backpack squats, and dumbbell deadlifts with single-leg hip hinges or glute bridges. Water bottles or filled bags work as light weights.

To make bodyweight moves harder, slow them down, add pauses, use one leg, or increase repetitions. These swaps keep your routine going when you travel or train at home.
