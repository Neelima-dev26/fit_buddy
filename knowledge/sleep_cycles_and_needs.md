# Sleep cycles and how much sleep you need
Category: wellness
Keywords: sleep, sleep cycles, how much sleep, REM, deep sleep, hours, teens, adults
Summary: Most adults need 7 or more hours, and teenagers need about 8 to 10.

Sleep moves through light, deep, and REM stages in cycles of about 90 minutes. Deep sleep supports physical recovery, and REM supports memory and mood. Adults generally need 7 or more hours of sleep each night, and teenagers about 8 to 10.

Consistency helps: go to bed and wake at similar times. If you sleep enough but feel tired all the time, snore loudly, or stop breathing at night, talk to a doctor, as these can be signs of a sleep disorder.
