# Improving general energy and health
Category: wellness, workout, meal
Keywords: energy, health, wellness, daily habits, feel better, fatigue
Summary: Movement, sleep, food, and stress habits all affect how energetic you feel.

Small daily habits help: walk for 10 to 30 minutes, drink water, eat regular balanced meals, get 7 or more hours of sleep, spend time outdoors, and take short breaks from screens. Strength exercises twice a week improve energy and everyday function.

Pick one habit to focus on for a week, then add another. If you feel unusually tired for a long time, see a doctor to check for other causes.
