# Quick and budget-friendly meals
Category: meal

Healthy eating does not need to be expensive or slow. Cheap staples include oats, rice, lentils, dried or canned beans, eggs, frozen vegetables, bananas, and seasonal fruit.

Quick ideas for 15 minutes or less: egg fried rice with frozen vegetables, bean and cheese quesadillas, oatmeal with a banana, dal with rice, a vegetable omelette, or pasta with canned tomatoes and beans.

Cooking a big pot of dal, chili, or soup and eating it for two or three days saves time. Chopping vegetables in advance and keeping frozen vegetables on hand makes a quick meal much easier.
