# Deload and recovery weeks
Category: general
Keywords: deload, recovery week, rest week, fatigue, overtraining, easier week
Summary: An easier week every few weeks helps your body recover and keep improving.

After 3 to 6 weeks of building up, many people take an easier week: reduce the weight or sets by about a third, add more walking and stretching, and focus on sleep and food. You are not losing progress, you are letting your body catch up.

Signs you may need extra recovery include persistent tiredness, poor sleep, low motivation, or soreness that does not fade. If these continue despite rest, consider speaking to a doctor.
