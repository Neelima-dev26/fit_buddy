# Fasting and Ramadan: general wellness tips
Category: general
Keywords: ramadan, fasting, iftar, suhoor, religious fasting, hydration, exercise
Summary: Plan balanced meals, hydrate between sunset and dawn, and adjust exercise during fasts.

At the meal before dawn (suhoor), include slow-digesting foods such as oats, whole grains, eggs, yogurt, and fruit. At sunset (iftar), break the fast gently with water and dates, then have a balanced meal of protein, vegetables, and grains. Drink plenty of fluids between meals and limit very salty or fried foods.

Many people exercise lightly after iftar, or before the meal in gentle form. Reduce intensity if you feel dizzy. People with medical conditions, such as diabetes, or those who are pregnant or on medication should talk to a doctor about fasting.
