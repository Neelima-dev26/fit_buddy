# Restarting after missed days
Category: wellness

Everyone misses days. Do not try to make up for missed sessions by doing double. Simply return to your normal plan.

After a short break of a few days, resume at your usual level. After a longer break, start lighter, perhaps 50 to 70 percent of your previous effort, and build up over one or two weeks.

Make restarting easy: pick a small first step like a 10-minute walk, put it in your calendar, and ignore the idea of a perfect streak. A streak that restarts is still progress.
