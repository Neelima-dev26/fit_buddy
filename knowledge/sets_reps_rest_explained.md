# Sets, reps, and rest explained
Category: general
Keywords: sets, reps, repetitions, rest, rest time, beginner terms
Summary: A rep is one full movement and a set is a group of reps.

A repetition (rep) is one complete movement, such as one squat. A set is a group of reps done without stopping, for example 10 squats. Rest is the pause between sets.

For beginners, 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps with 60 to 90 seconds of rest is a good starting point. Fewer reps with heavier weight build strength, while more reps with lighter weight build endurance. Choose a weight that makes the last two reps challenging with good form.
