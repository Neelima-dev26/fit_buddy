# Motivation and plateaus
Category: wellness

Motivation naturally rises and falls, so build habits that do not rely on it. Schedule workouts like appointments, prepare your clothes in advance, and choose activities you enjoy.

A plateau, when progress seems to stall, is normal. Try changing your routine, increasing the challenge slightly, sleeping more, and checking you are recovering well. Track things beyond the scale, such as strength, energy, and mood.

Take a lighter week when you feel worn out. Small steady steps lead to lasting results.
