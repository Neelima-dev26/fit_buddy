# Pescatarian meal ideas
Category: meal
Keywords: pescatarian, fish, seafood, vegetarian plus fish, omega-3
Summary: A pescatarian pattern adds fish and seafood to a vegetarian diet.

Ideas: baked salmon with vegetables and rice, tuna and chickpea salad, shrimp stir-fry with vegetables, fish tacos with cabbage slaw, lentil soup with a side of sardines on toast, or grilled fish with quinoa.

Fish provides protein and omega-3 fats, and varying your choices helps. Choose a mix of fish types, and ask a doctor if you are pregnant or have special needs, since some fish are advised against.
