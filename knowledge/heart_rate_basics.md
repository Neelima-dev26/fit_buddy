# Heart rate basics for exercise
Category: general
Keywords: heart rate, max heart rate, zones, 220 minus age, pulse, tracker
Summary: Heart rate gives a rough guide to intensity, but the talk test works well too.

A common rough estimate of maximum heart rate is 220 minus your age. Moderate intensity is often described as about 50 to 70 percent of that, and vigorous as about 70 to 85 percent. These are only estimates, and individuals vary a lot.

You can check your pulse at your wrist or use a fitness tracker. If you take medication that affects your heart rate or have a heart condition, talk to a doctor about safe targets before relying on these numbers.
