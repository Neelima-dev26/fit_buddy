# Time outdoors and daylight
Category: wellness
Keywords: outdoors, sunlight, nature, daylight, walking outside, mood, sleep
Summary: Daylight and time in nature can lift mood and support sleep.

Aim to get outside for even 10 to 20 minutes a day, ideally in the morning or at lunch. Walk, stretch, or have a break outdoors. Morning light helps regulate your body clock, and green spaces can reduce stress.

Protect your skin with sunscreen or shade during strong sun, and dress for the weather. On rainy or cold days, sit by a window or use indoor activities.
