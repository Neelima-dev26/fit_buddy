# Building healthy habits
Category: wellness

Small, specific habits are easier to keep than big changes. Start with something so easy you can do it on a bad day, such as stretching for two minutes or taking a 10-minute walk.

Link the habit to something you already do, for example stretching after brushing your teeth or walking after lunch. Put reminders where you will see them, and track your progress with a checklist, which makes progress visible and motivating.

Aim for consistency, not perfection. If you miss a day, simply restart the next day. Increase the habit slowly once it feels automatic, and celebrate small wins.
