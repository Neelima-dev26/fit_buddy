# Calf and ankle exercises
Category: form
Keywords: calf raise, ankle, balance, lower leg, running
Summary: Strong calves and ankles support walking, running, and balance.

Calf raises: stand with your hands on a wall, rise onto your toes, pause, and lower slowly. Do 2 to 3 sets of 12 to 15, or try one leg at a time for more challenge. Ankle circles and alphabet tracing with your toes help mobility. Balancing on one leg trains the small stabilizing muscles.

Stretch your calves after exercise by pressing your heel to the floor with the knee straight, then bent. Build up gradually if you are starting to run, since calves and ankles need time to adapt.
