# A 15-minute home workout with no equipment
Category: workout

When you are short on time, a quick circuit still helps. Warm up for 2 minutes with marching and arm circles.

Do each move for 40 seconds with 20 seconds of rest: bodyweight squats, incline or knee push-ups, reverse lunges, plank, glute bridges, and march or jumping jacks. Repeat the circuit twice, taking 1 minute of rest between rounds. Finish with 2 minutes of stretching.

If 40 seconds is too long, use 30 seconds of work and 30 seconds of rest. Do this circuit three days a week, or on busy days instead of skipping exercise altogether.
