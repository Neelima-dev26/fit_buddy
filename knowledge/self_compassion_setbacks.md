# Self-compassion after setbacks
Category: wellness
Keywords: setback, missed workout, guilt, motivation, self-compassion, restart, perfectionism
Summary: Being kind to yourself helps you get back on track faster.

Everyone has off days. Instead of criticism, talk to yourself the way you would to a friend: notice what happened, learn from it, and choose a small next step. Perfectionism often leads to giving up, while flexibility helps habits last.

Plan for obstacles: have easy versions of workouts for busy or low-energy days. A missed day is just a missed day, and you can restart at the next opportunity.
