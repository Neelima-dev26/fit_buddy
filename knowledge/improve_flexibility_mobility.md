# Improving flexibility and mobility
Category: workout, wellness
Keywords: flexibility, mobility, stretching, range of motion, tight hips, hamstrings
Summary: Regular gentle stretching and mobility work improve range of motion.

Stretch most days, ideally when your muscles are warm. Hold each stretch for 20 to 30 seconds and repeat two or three times. Focus on the hips, hamstrings, calves, chest, and shoulders if you sit a lot. Yoga and mobility flows combine stretching with strength.

Progress is gradual and takes weeks. Stretch to mild tension, never sharp pain, and breathe steadily. Dynamic movements such as leg swings are best before exercise, and static stretches work well afterward.
