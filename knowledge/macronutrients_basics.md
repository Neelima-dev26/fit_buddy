# Macronutrients: carbs, protein, and fat
Category: general
Keywords: macros, carbohydrates, protein, fat, nutrients, energy
Summary: Carbohydrates, protein, and fat each have a job, and a balanced diet includes all three.

Carbohydrates are the body's main fuel and come from grains, fruit, vegetables, beans, and dairy. Protein helps build and repair muscle and comes from foods such as eggs, fish, meat, dairy, beans, lentils, tofu, and nuts. Fats support hormones and absorb some vitamins, and healthy sources include olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado, and fish.

Most people do well eating a variety of whole foods without counting grams. If you have a medical condition or special needs, a registered dietitian can provide a personal plan.
