# Eating to fit cultural and religious preferences
Category: general
Keywords: halal, kosher, jain, vegetarian, cultural food, religious dietary, preferences
Summary: Healthy eating works within many cultural and religious traditions.

Whether you follow halal, kosher, Jain, vegetarian, or other food customs, the same principles apply: a variety of vegetables, fruit, whole grains, and proteins that fit your tradition, with fewer heavily processed foods.

Tell FitBuddy your restrictions in your profile so ideas fit your needs, and always read ingredient labels. A registered dietitian can help build a plan that respects your values.
