# A positive relationship with exercise and your body
Category: wellness
Keywords: body image, self-esteem, comparison, social media, appearance, confidence
Summary: Focus on what your body can do and how you feel, not how it looks.

Set goals around strength, energy, mood, and enjoyment. Notice progress such as walking farther or lifting more. Limit social media that makes you feel worse, and remember that online images are often edited.

Treat exercise as care, not punishment, and avoid using it to make up for eating. If thoughts about your body or food cause distress, please talk to a trusted person, a doctor, or a mental health professional.
