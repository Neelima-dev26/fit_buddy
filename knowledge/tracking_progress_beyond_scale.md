# Tracking progress beyond the scale
Category: wellness

Body weight tells only part of the story. Other signs of progress include more energy, better sleep, improved mood, being able to walk or exercise longer, lifting heavier weights or doing more repetitions, easier daily tasks, and clothes fitting more comfortably.

Keep a simple record of these, such as your workouts, sleep hours, mood, and habits, which FitBuddy can help you log. Review them weekly to see patterns.

If focusing on numbers makes you anxious, concentrate on habits and how you feel instead.
