# How long should a workout be?
Category: general
Keywords: duration, how long, 20 minutes, 30 minutes, 45 minutes, session length
Summary: A good beginner session lasts 20 to 45 minutes, and shorter sessions count too.

Most beginners do well with 20 to 45 minutes including a warm-up and cool-down. Strength sessions are often 30 to 45 minutes, and a brisk walk of 20 to 30 minutes is plenty.

Quality matters more than length. If you only have 10 minutes, a short circuit is still worth doing. If a long session leaves you exhausted or sore for days, shorten it. You can build up gradually as your fitness improves.
