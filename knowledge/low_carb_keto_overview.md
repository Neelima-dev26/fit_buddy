# Low-carb and keto diets: an overview
Category: general
Keywords: keto, low carb, ketogenic, atkins, diet trend, carbohydrates
Summary: Low-carb diets reduce carbohydrates, and they may not suit everyone.

Low-carb and ketogenic diets limit foods such as grains, fruit, and starchy vegetables and increase fats and proteins. Some people find them helpful, but they can be hard to maintain, may lack fibre and some nutrients, and can affect energy during exercise.

They are not suitable for everyone, such as people with certain medical conditions or on some medicines. Whatever diet you consider, a doctor or registered dietitian can advise on safety and nutrition, and a balanced pattern with whole foods works well for most people.
