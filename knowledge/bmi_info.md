# About BMI
Category: general

Body mass index (BMI) is weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. For adults, the commonly used reference ranges are below 18.5, 18.5 to 24.9, 25 to 29.9, and 30 or above.

BMI is a quick screening number only. It does not measure body fat directly, and it ignores muscle mass, bone structure, age, sex, and where weight is carried. A muscular person can have a high BMI without excess body fat. It is not a diagnosis and should not be used as a personal goal. For teenagers, BMI is interpreted with age and sex-specific charts by a healthcare professional.
