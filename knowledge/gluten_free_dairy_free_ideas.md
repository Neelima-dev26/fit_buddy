# Gluten-free and dairy-free ideas
Category: meal

Many everyday foods are naturally gluten-free: rice, quinoa, potatoes, corn, lentils and dal, beans, eggs, fish, meat, fruit, and vegetables. Look for oats and sauces labeled gluten-free if you need to avoid gluten completely.

Naturally dairy-free options include fruit, vegetables, grains, beans, nuts, and most meat and fish. Plant milks, coconut yogurt, and tofu can replace dairy. Choose fortified plant milks and calcium-set tofu to help with calcium.

Always read labels, since gluten and milk can hide in sauces and processed foods. If you have celiac disease, an allergy, or an intolerance, a doctor or registered dietitian can give you personal advice.
