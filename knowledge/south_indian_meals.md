# South Indian meal ideas
Category: meal
Keywords: south indian, idli, dosa, sambar, rasam, curd rice, upma, millet
Summary: South Indian cuisine offers many light, vegetable-rich, protein-friendly meals.

Breakfast: idli or dosa with sambar and chutney, ragi dosa, vegetable upma, or pongal. Lunch: rice with sambar or rasam, a vegetable poriyal, and curd. Dinner: lighter options such as millet roti with a vegetable kootu, or dosa with a lentil side.

Sambar and dals add protein and fibre, and fermented foods like idli, dosa, and curd are staples. Add vegetables to batter or sides and enjoy coconut chutneys in moderation.
