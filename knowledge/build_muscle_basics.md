# Building muscle: the basics
Category: general
Keywords: muscle, hypertrophy, strength, gain muscle, protein, progressive overload
Summary: Muscle grows when you challenge it regularly, eat enough, and recover well.

To build muscle, train each major muscle group two or three times a week with 2 to 4 sets of 6 to 12 reps, and gradually increase the challenge. Eat regular meals with protein foods, get enough sleep, and be patient: visible changes often take weeks to months.

Good form and consistent progress matter more than heavy weights. If you are under 18 or have health conditions, check with a doctor or qualified coach before heavy lifting.
