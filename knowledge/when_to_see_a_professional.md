# When to see a professional
Category: general

Check with a doctor before starting an exercise program if you have a heart, lung, kidney, or metabolic condition such as diabetes, are pregnant or recently gave birth, have joint or back problems, are recovering from an injury or surgery, take regular medication, or have not been active for a long time and have health concerns.

Stop exercising and seek medical help right away for chest pain, severe shortness of breath, fainting, or sudden severe pain. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian for personal nutrition plans, weight goals, or eating concerns, and to a physical therapist for ongoing pain or injuries.

FitBuddy shares general information only and cannot replace professional advice.
