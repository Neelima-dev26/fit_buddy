# Meal prep plan: high-protein week
Category: meal
Keywords: meal prep, high protein, chicken, tofu, eggs, paneer, lentils, gym
Summary: A sample prep session for people who want protein at every meal.

Bake or grill a batch of chicken, fish, tofu, or paneer, boil a dozen eggs, and cook a pot of lentils or beans plus a grain. Roast a tray of vegetables and prepare Greek yogurt or cottage cheese for snacks.

Assemble bowls: grain, protein, vegetables, and a sauce such as yogurt-lemon or tahini. Keep boiled eggs, yogurt with fruit, roasted chickpeas, and edamame as ready snacks. Eat fish and chicken within 3 days, and freeze portions you will not use in time.
