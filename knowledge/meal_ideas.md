# Simple meal and snack ideas
Category: meal

Breakfast: oatmeal with fruit and nuts, Greek yogurt with berries, scrambled eggs with vegetables on whole-grain toast, or a tofu scramble. Check labels and swap ingredients for allergies or restrictions.

Lunch: a salad with beans or chicken, a whole-grain wrap with hummus and vegetables, lentil soup with bread, or leftovers from dinner.

Dinner: a stir-fry with vegetables, tofu or chicken and rice, baked fish with potatoes and green beans, a vegetable and bean chili, or whole-wheat pasta with a vegetable sauce.

Snacks: fruit with nut butter, carrots with hummus, a handful of nuts, yogurt, or a boiled egg. Vegetarian and vegan options include beans, lentils, tofu, tempeh, and nuts. Preparing a few meals or ingredients in advance makes healthy choices easier on busy days.
