# Screen breaks and posture
Category: wellness

Long hours at a screen can strain your eyes, neck, and back. Try the 20-20-20 idea: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Set your screen near eye level, keep your feet flat on the floor, and keep your back supported. Stand up, stretch, or walk for a minute or two every hour.

Add a few shoulder rolls, neck stretches, and a short walk to your day. If you have ongoing neck or back pain, see a doctor or physical therapist.
