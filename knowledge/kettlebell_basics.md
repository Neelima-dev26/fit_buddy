# Kettlebell basics
Category: workout
Keywords: kettlebell, swing, goblet squat, carry, beginner
Summary: Kettlebells are a compact tool for strength and conditioning.

Beginner-friendly kettlebell moves include the goblet squat, deadlift, farmer's carry (walking while holding a kettlebell), and the swing. Learn the hip hinge first, since the swing comes from your hips, not your arms.

Start with a lighter weight and consider a lesson with a qualified trainer or a reputable beginner video for the swing. Keep your back flat, and stop if you feel strain in your lower back.
