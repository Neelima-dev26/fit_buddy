# Plank and core holds: form guide
Category: form
Keywords: plank, side plank, dead bug, bird dog, core, abs, hold
Summary: Core holds build trunk stability for better posture and stronger lifts.

For a plank, rest on your forearms or hands with your body in a straight line from head to heels. Squeeze your glutes, brace your core as if preparing for a light punch, and breathe steadily. Do not let your hips sag or pike up.

Beginners can start with 15 to 20 seconds on their knees, then work toward 30 to 60 seconds on their toes. Add side planks, dead bugs, and bird dogs for variety. Do 2 to 3 holds, and stop a hold as soon as your form breaks, since a shorter good hold beats a long sloppy one.
