# Meal prep plan: budget and student week
Category: meal
Keywords: meal prep, budget, student, cheap, rice, beans, lentils, frozen vegetables
Summary: Cheap staples can cover a whole week of meals.

Build the week on rice, oats, lentils, canned or dried beans, eggs, frozen vegetables, bananas, and seasonal fruit. Cook a big pot of dal or bean chili, a pot of rice, and a tray of roasted vegetables. Make overnight oats for breakfasts.

Turn the same base into different meals: add curry spices one day, salsa and tortillas another, and a fried-rice style stir-fry on a third. Shop with a list, buy staples in bulk, and freeze leftovers in single portions.
