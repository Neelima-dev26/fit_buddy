# Burnout and work-life balance
Category: wellness
Keywords: burnout, work stress, overwork, balance, breaks, boundaries, exhaustion
Summary: Rest, boundaries, and breaks prevent exhaustion.

Signs of burnout include constant exhaustion, feeling detached, and reduced performance. Protect your time with regular breaks, set a clear end to your workday, take your days off, and make time for movement and hobbies.

Talk to your manager or someone you trust about workload. If you feel overwhelmed, hopeless, or unwell for weeks, see a doctor or mental health professional.
