# Habit stacking and cues
Category: wellness
Keywords: habit, habit stacking, cue, routine, tiny habits, consistency, reminders
Summary: Attach a new habit to something you already do.

Link a small new action to an existing routine: stretch after brushing your teeth, drink a glass of water after your morning coffee, or walk after lunch. Keep the new habit tiny, such as two minutes, so it is easy to start.

Make cues visible, like leaving your shoes by the door, and reward yourself with a tick on your checklist. Increase the habit slowly once it feels automatic.
