# Stress, mood, and movement
Category: wellness

Regular movement can lift mood and help you feel less stressed. Even a 10-minute walk outside can help. Gentle activities such as stretching, yoga, or easy cycling are good choices on stressful days.

Slow breathing is a quick reset: breathe in through your nose for about 4 seconds, then out slowly for about 6 seconds, for one to three minutes. Short breaks from screens, time outdoors, and talking with a friend also help.

Try to keep consistent sleep and meal times. If stress, low mood, or anxiety is lasting or getting in the way of daily life, please talk to a doctor or mental health professional.
