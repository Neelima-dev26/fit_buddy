# Meal prep basics
Category: meal

Preparing food in advance makes healthy eating easier on busy days. Choose one or two hours a week to cook a grain such as rice or quinoa, a protein such as lentils, beans, chicken, tofu, or eggs, and a tray of roasted vegetables. Mix and match them through the week as bowls, wraps, or salads.

Cool food quickly, store it in covered containers in the refrigerator, and eat it within about 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot. Freeze extra portions of soups, curries, and chilis for later.

Wash and chop fruit and vegetables ahead so they are ready to grab for snacks.
