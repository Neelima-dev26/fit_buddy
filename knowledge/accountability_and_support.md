# Accountability and support
Category: wellness

Support makes habits stick. Exercise with a friend, join a class or walking group, or share your weekly goals with someone who will check in.

Tell family or housemates what you are working on, and ask for their help, such as protecting your workout time. Online communities and fitness apps can also provide encouragement.

Choose supportive people over critical ones, and remember that comparing yourself with others rarely helps. Your own progress is what matters.
