# Setting goals that work
Category: wellness

Clear goals are easier to follow. Use the SMART idea: specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound. Instead of "get fit", try "walk for 20 minutes on four days a week for the next month".

Start with small goals, track them, and review each week. Celebrate progress such as consistency, energy, or sleep, not just outcomes. If a goal feels too hard, make it smaller instead of giving up.

Write goals down and tell someone. Adjust them as life changes, since flexible goals last longer.
