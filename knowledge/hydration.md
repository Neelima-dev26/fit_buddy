# Hydration
Category: meal, wellness

Water supports energy, digestion, and temperature control. A common rule of thumb is about 8 glasses of fluid a day, but needs vary with body size, climate, and activity, so treat it as a rough guide. Drinking when you are thirsty and aiming for pale yellow urine are simple signals that you are doing well.

Drink a glass of water with each meal and keep a bottle nearby during the day. Drink a little before, during, and after exercise, especially when it is hot. Fruit, vegetables, milk, tea, and soup also contribute to your fluids. Plain water is a good everyday choice.
