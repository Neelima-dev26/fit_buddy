# Fitness trackers and step goals
Category: general
Keywords: tracker, smartwatch, steps, 10000 steps, step goal, pedometer
Summary: Trackers can motivate you, but they are a guide, not a rulebook.

Many people use 10,000 steps as a target, but any increase in daily steps is beneficial. Check your current average and add 500 to 1,000 steps a day for a week or two.

Trackers estimate steps, heart rate, and sleep, but they are not perfectly accurate, so look at trends over time. If numbers make you anxious or obsessive, turn off some notifications and focus on how you feel.
