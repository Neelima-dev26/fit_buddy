# Cycling and swimming basics
Category: workout

Both are low-impact activities that are easy on the joints.

Cycling: set the seat so your knee is slightly bent at the bottom of the pedal stroke, wear a helmet, and start with 15 to 20 minutes at an easy pace. A stationary bike is a good choice for beginners and bad weather.

Swimming: start with short stretches and rest between laps, building up to 20 to 30 minutes. Swim in pools with a lifeguard, never swim alone, and learn basic water safety. If you cannot swim well, water walking or water aerobics in shallow water is a great alternative.
