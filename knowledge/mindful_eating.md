# Mindful eating
Category: wellness, general
Keywords: mindful eating, eat slowly, hunger cues, fullness, distractions
Summary: Paying attention to food helps you enjoy it and notice fullness.

Try eating without screens, take a breath before you start, chew slowly, and notice flavors and textures. Pause halfway through a meal to check how hungry or full you feel.

Mindful eating is about awareness, not rules. Eat when you are hungry, stop when you are satisfied, and be kind to yourself on days when you eat quickly or emotionally.
