# Example weekly workout schedules
Category: workout

Three days: Monday, Wednesday, and Friday full-body strength sessions, with easy walks on Tuesday and Saturday and Sunday as a full rest day.

Four days: Monday upper body, Tuesday lower body, Thursday upper body, and Friday lower body, with a walk or stretching on the other days.

Five days: Monday strength, Tuesday walk or cycling, Wednesday strength, Thursday yoga or mobility, Friday strength, and a longer walk on Saturday, with Sunday for rest.

Pick the schedule you can keep up. Consistency matters more than doing a lot. Always include at least one full rest day.
