# Morning or evening workouts?
Category: general
Keywords: morning, evening, best time to exercise, schedule, sleep
Summary: The best time to exercise is the time you can stick with consistently.

Morning exercise can build a routine before the day gets busy, while evening sessions can be more energetic and help release stress. Both are fine for most people.

Very intense exercise close to bedtime can make it harder to sleep for some people, so try finishing hard workouts a few hours before bed and choose gentle stretching in the evening. Pick a time that fits your life, and keep it consistent.
