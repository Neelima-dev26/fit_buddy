# Indoor workouts for bad weather
Category: workout
Keywords: indoor, rain, bad weather, home workout, stairs, dance
Summary: When the weather is bad, indoor options keep your habit going.

Try a 15 to 30 minute bodyweight circuit, a yoga or stretching session, dancing to your favorite playlist, stair climbing, or marching in place while watching a show. Walking laps inside a mall or gym works well too.

Have a go-to plan ready so bad weather does not break the routine: for example squats, push-ups, lunges, and planks for 20 minutes. If you usually run or walk outside, a short indoor session still counts toward your weekly activity.
