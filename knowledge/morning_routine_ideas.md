# Morning routine ideas
Category: wellness

A simple morning routine can set a positive tone. Try drinking a glass of water, opening the curtains or going outside for daylight, doing five minutes of stretching or a short walk, and eating a breakfast with some protein and fibre.

Take a minute to decide your top one or two priorities for the day. Keep the routine short and realistic so you can stick with it, even on busy mornings.

Prepare the night before, with clothes out and breakfast ingredients ready, to make mornings smoother. Stretching is a good habit to tick off in your daily checklist.
