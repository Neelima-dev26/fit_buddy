# How to adjust your workout when you are sore
Category: workout
Keywords: sore, soreness, stiff, recovery day, active recovery, adjust
Summary: Mild soreness is normal, and light movement often helps it feel better.

If your muscles are sore, train a different area, such as upper body after a leg day, or choose gentle movement like walking, cycling, swimming, or stretching. Reduce the weight and range of motion if needed.

Sore is different from hurt: a dull ache that eases as you warm up is usually fine, while sharp, pinching, or joint pain means stop. Give very sore muscles an extra day, drink water, and sleep well. If pain is severe or does not improve in a few days, see a professional.
