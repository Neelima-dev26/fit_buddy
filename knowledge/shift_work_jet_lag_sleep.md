# Sleep tips for shift work and jet lag
Category: wellness
Keywords: shift work, night shift, jet lag, travel, sleep schedule, time zones
Summary: Light, timing, and routines help your body clock adjust.

For night shifts, keep the bedroom dark, wear sunglasses on the way home, use blackout curtains, and keep a regular sleep window. A short nap before a shift can help alertness.

For jet lag, shift your sleep time a little before travel, get daylight at your new destination, stay hydrated, and avoid heavy meals and alcohol on the flight. Adjustment often takes about a day per time zone.
