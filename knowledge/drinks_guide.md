# Drinks guide
Category: general

Water is a great everyday choice. Unsweetened tea, coffee in moderation, milk or fortified plant milk, and sparkling water are also fine. Add lemon, cucumber, mint, or fruit slices to water for flavor.

Sugary drinks such as soda, sweetened juices, and energy drinks are best kept for occasional treats. Caffeine can affect sleep, so many people avoid it in the afternoon and evening. Energy drinks are not recommended for teenagers.

Carry a water bottle, drink a glass with each meal, and sip before, during, and after exercise.
