# Social connection and wellbeing
Category: wellness
Keywords: friends, social, loneliness, community, support, group exercise
Summary: Staying connected supports both mental and physical health.

Spend time with people you trust, call a friend, join a group class, walk with a neighbor, or volunteer. Even short, regular contact helps.

If you feel lonely, start small, such as a message or a short coffee. Group activities double as exercise and motivation. If loneliness feels heavy or persistent, speaking to a counselor can help.
