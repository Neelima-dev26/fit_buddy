# Reading food labels
Category: general

Labels help you compare products. Start with the serving size, because all the numbers on the label refer to it.

The ingredient list is ordered by weight, so the first few items make up most of the product. Look for short ingredient lists with foods you recognize. Compare similar products for added sugar, salt (sodium), and fibre, and check for allergens, which are usually highlighted.

Claims on the front of a package, such as natural or light, are marketing, so check the back. You do not need to analyze every label. A few quick comparisons while shopping are enough.
