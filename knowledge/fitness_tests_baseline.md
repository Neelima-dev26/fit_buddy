# Simple fitness tests to track progress
Category: general
Keywords: fitness test, baseline, push-up test, plank test, walk test, measure progress
Summary: Simple tests every few weeks show your progress without the scale.

Try these every 4 to 6 weeks: how many push-ups you can do with good form (any version), how long you can hold a plank, how many sit-to-stand repetitions you can do in 30 seconds, how far or fast you can walk in 10 minutes, and how you feel on a flight of stairs.

Write the results down and repeat under similar conditions, such as the same time of day. Progress here shows real improvement in strength and endurance.
