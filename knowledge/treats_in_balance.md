# Enjoying treats in balance
Category: general
Keywords: dessert, sweets, treats, chocolate, cravings, sugar, moderation
Summary: All foods can fit, and enjoying treats mindfully is part of healthy eating.

Include treats you really enjoy and savor them without distractions. Having them with or after a meal can feel more satisfying than eating them when very hungry. Fruit with yogurt or dark chocolate with nuts can be a middle ground.

Cravings are normal. Notice if you reach for sweets when tired or stressed, and try sleep, a walk, or a balanced snack first. Avoid labeling yourself good or bad after eating.
