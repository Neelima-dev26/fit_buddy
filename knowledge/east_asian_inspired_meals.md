# East Asian inspired meals
Category: meal

Many East Asian dishes are built around rice or noodles, vegetables, and a protein.

Ideas: a rice bowl with tofu or egg and sauteed vegetables, a vegetable and chicken stir-fry, miso soup with tofu and greens, steamed fish with ginger and bok choy, congee with an egg and spring onion, a vegetable noodle soup, or edamame as a snack.

Soy sauce and some sauces are salty, so use them lightly and add flavor with ginger, garlic, spring onion, and lime. Brown rice or mixed grains add fibre.
