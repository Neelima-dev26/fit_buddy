# Balanced eating basics
Category: meal

A simple way to think about a meal is to fill about half your plate with vegetables and fruit, about a quarter with protein foods, and about a quarter with whole grains or other starchy foods. Add a small amount of healthy fats such as olive oil, nuts, or avocado.

Protein foods include beans, lentils, tofu, eggs, fish, poultry, lean meat, yogurt, and nuts. Whole grains include oats, brown rice, whole-wheat bread, and quinoa. Aim for a variety of colorful vegetables across the week.

Eating regular meals, drinking mostly water, and limiting heavily processed foods and sugary drinks are helpful habits. There is no single perfect diet, so choose foods you enjoy and can keep up. For personal nutrition advice, especially for medical conditions or allergies, see a doctor or registered dietitian.

Reference: general guidance from national healthy-eating guides such as MyPlate.
