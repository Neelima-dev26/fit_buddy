# Balance and strength for adults over 50
Category: workout, general

Strength and balance exercises help keep you steady and independent. Aim for muscle-strengthening activities two or more days a week, plus balance practice.

Easy options: sit-to-stand from a sturdy chair, wall push-ups, heel raises while holding a counter, standing on one leg near a support, heel-to-toe walking, and gentle band rows. Do 1 to 2 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions and add more over time.

Keep a chair or counter nearby for safety, move slowly, and wear supportive shoes. Talk to a doctor before starting if you have health conditions, take medication, or have had falls or dizziness.
