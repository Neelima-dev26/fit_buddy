# Vegetarian and vegan meal ideas
Category: meal

Plenty of filling meals need no meat. Try lentil or bean curry with rice, chickpea and vegetable stew, tofu or tempeh stir-fry, a veggie burrito bowl with beans and corn, hummus and vegetable wraps, or pasta with a vegetable and bean sauce.

Breakfast ideas: oatmeal with fruit and seeds, a tofu scramble, peanut butter on whole-grain toast, or a smoothie with fruit and soy or other plant milk.

Beans, lentils, tofu, tempeh, nuts, seeds, and whole grains are good protein sources. Vegans may want to talk to a doctor or dietitian about vitamin B12, since it is mostly found in animal foods or fortified products.
