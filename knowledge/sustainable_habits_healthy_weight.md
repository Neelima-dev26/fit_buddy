# Sustainable habits for a healthy weight
Category: general, meal
Keywords: weight, fat loss, healthy weight, sustainable, habits, gradual, goals
Summary: Small, steady habits work better than crash plans.

Instead of focusing only on the scale, build habits you can keep: regular movement you enjoy, meals with vegetables, protein, and whole grains, enough sleep, and ways to manage stress. Gradual change is safer and easier to maintain than extreme dieting.

Weight is only one measure of health, and it varies day to day. A doctor or registered dietitian can help set a personal, healthy goal, especially if you are under 18, have a health condition, or have struggled with disordered eating.
