# Short on time: 10 and 20 minute workouts
Category: workout
Keywords: short workout, 10 minute, 20 minute, quick, time, busy, circuit
Summary: Short sessions still count, and any minutes of activity add up.

Physical activity guidelines count all minutes of activity, so there is no minimum bout length. A 10-minute circuit still helps: 40 seconds each of squats, push-ups, reverse lunges, and a plank, with 20 seconds rest, repeated twice.

For 20 minutes, add a 3-minute warm-up and do 3 rounds of 4 to 5 exercises. You can also split a workout into two 10-minute blocks, morning and evening. Choose full-body moves that use big muscles to get the most from limited time.
