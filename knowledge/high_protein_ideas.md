# High-protein meal and snack ideas
Category: meal

Protein-rich foods help you feel full and support muscle repair after exercise. Good choices include eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese or paneer, lentils and dal, chickpeas, beans, tofu and tempeh, fish, chicken, lean meat, and nuts and seeds.

Easy ideas: scrambled eggs on whole-grain toast, Greek yogurt with fruit and nuts, a lentil soup, a chickpea salad, tofu stir-fry with vegetables, a grilled chicken or paneer wrap, or tuna with whole-grain crackers.

Spread protein across the day instead of having it all at one meal. You can combine plant proteins, such as rice with dal or hummus with whole-grain bread, for a satisfying meal.
