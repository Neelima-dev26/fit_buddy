# Plant protein guide
Category: meal

You can get plenty of protein from plants. Good sources are lentils, chickpeas, beans, peas, tofu, tempeh, edamame, soy milk, quinoa, nuts, seeds, and whole grains.

Eating a variety through the day covers your needs. Classic pairings include rice with dal, beans with corn tortillas, and hummus with whole-grain bread. Add seeds to oatmeal and salads, and add beans or lentils to soups and curries.

If you follow a vegan diet, talk to a doctor or dietitian about nutrients such as vitamin B12, iron, and calcium.
