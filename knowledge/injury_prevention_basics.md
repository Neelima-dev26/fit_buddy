# Preventing exercise injuries
Category: general

Most beginner injuries come from doing too much too soon. Progress gradually, increasing time or intensity by small steps each week.

Warm up before and cool down after exercise, wear supportive footwear, and learn good technique before adding weight. Include rest days, vary your activities, and sleep enough, since tired muscles are more prone to strain.

Muscle soreness is normal, but sharp pain, joint pain, or pain that gets worse is not. Stop the exercise and rest. If pain lasts more than a few days, see a doctor or physical therapist before returning to training.
