# Weekly check-in and reflection prompts
Category: wellness
Keywords: weekly review, reflection, check-in, goals, progress, reflect
Summary: A short weekly reflection keeps your plan realistic and motivating.

Once a week, ask yourself: What went well? What was hard? How did my sleep, mood, and energy look? What is one small thing I will do next week? Is my plan realistic, or should I make it easier or change it?

Look at your tracker, such as workouts, sleep hours, water, and mood, and notice patterns. Celebrate any progress, and adjust the plan rather than blaming yourself.
