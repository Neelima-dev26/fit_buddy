# Breakfast ideas collection
Category: meal
Keywords: breakfast, morning meal, quick breakfast, high protein breakfast, oats, eggs, idli, poha
Summary: Breakfast can be quick, filling, and varied.

Quick: overnight oats with fruit, Greek yogurt with nuts and berries, peanut butter on whole-grain toast with a banana, or a fruit and yogurt smoothie.

Cooked: vegetable omelette with toast, egg bhurji with roti, paneer or tofu scramble, idli or dosa with sambar, vegetable upma, poha with peanuts, moong dal chilla, or oatmeal with seeds.

Aim to combine some protein, fibre, and fruit or vegetables so you feel satisfied. If you are not hungry in the morning, a small snack later is fine.
