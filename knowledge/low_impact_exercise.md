# Low-impact exercise options
Category: workout

Low-impact exercise is gentler on joints such as the knees, hips, and ankles. Good choices include brisk walking, cycling, swimming or water aerobics, an elliptical trainer, a rowing machine, chair exercises, and gentle yoga.

If your knees feel sore, try shallow squats to a chair, glute bridges, step-ups on a low step, and leg raises. Strong hips and thighs help support the knees. Choose supportive shoes and a soft surface when possible.

Start with 10 to 20 minutes and build slowly. Stop any movement that causes sharp or lasting pain. If joint pain continues, see a doctor or physical therapist.
