# Japanese and Korean inspired meal ideas
Category: meal
Keywords: japanese, korean, sushi, miso soup, bibimbap, kimchi, rice bowl, tofu
Summary: Rice bowls, soups, and vegetable sides make balanced meals.

Ideas: a rice bowl with salmon or tofu and vegetables, miso soup with greens, bibimbap with sauteed vegetables and an egg, a vegetable and tofu stew, grilled fish with rice and pickled vegetables, or a simple omelette roll.

Fermented foods like kimchi and miso add flavor. Soy sauce and some sauces are salty, so use them lightly, and add vegetables to fill the plate.
