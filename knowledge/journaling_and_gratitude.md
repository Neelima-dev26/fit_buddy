# Journaling and gratitude
Category: wellness
Keywords: journaling, gratitude, reflection, writing, mood, mental health
Summary: Writing for a few minutes a day can clear your mind and improve mood.

Try a simple routine: write three things that went well today, one thing you are grateful for, and one small thing you want to do tomorrow. You can also write freely for five minutes about what is on your mind.

There is no right or wrong way, and it can be done on paper or on your phone. Pair it with a habit you already have, such as before bed.
