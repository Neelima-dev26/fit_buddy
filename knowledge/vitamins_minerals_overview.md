# Vitamins and minerals: food first
Category: general
Keywords: vitamins, minerals, iron, calcium, vitamin D, B12, deficiency, nutrients
Summary: A varied diet usually provides what you need, and supplements are for specific situations.

Eating a colorful range of vegetables and fruit, whole grains, beans, dairy or fortified alternatives, and proteins covers most needs. Some nutrients need extra attention: iron (beans, leafy greens, meat), calcium (dairy, fortified milks, tofu), vitamin D (sunlight, fortified foods, fish), and vitamin B12 (animal foods or fortified foods, important for vegans).

If you suspect a deficiency, such as constant tiredness, see a doctor for a blood test instead of guessing.
