# Swapping between dumbbells, bands, and machines
Category: workout
Keywords: swap, dumbbell, resistance band, machine, substitute, equipment
Summary: You can keep the same routine with different equipment.

A dumbbell row can become a banded row, a dumbbell press a band chest press, and goblet squats banded squats. Machines such as the leg press, seated row, and chest press are similar to their free-weight versions but guide the movement.

When swapping, aim for a similar effort: the last two repetitions should feel challenging. Bands give more resistance at the top of a movement, while dumbbells are constant, so use control. Keep the number of sets and repetitions the same, and increase difficulty gradually.
