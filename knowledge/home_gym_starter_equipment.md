# Home gym starter equipment
Category: general
Keywords: home gym, equipment, dumbbells, resistance bands, mat, jump rope, what to buy
Summary: A few inexpensive items go a long way.

Good starter items are a pair or set of adjustable dumbbells, a few resistance bands, an exercise mat, and a sturdy chair or bench. A jump rope, a pull-up bar, and a foam roller can come later.

You do not need everything at once. Many people get great results from bodyweight exercises and one set of dumbbells. Buy items you will use, check weight limits, and look for second-hand equipment to save money.
