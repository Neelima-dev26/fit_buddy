# Push and pull progressions
Category: form

Progress step by step so each stage feels challenging but doable.

Push-up progression: wall push-ups, then incline push-ups on a counter or sturdy table, then knee push-ups, then full push-ups. Move up when you can complete 3 sets of 10 with good form.

Pulling progression: bent-over dumbbell rows or resistance band rows, then inverted rows under a sturdy table or bar if you have one, then band-assisted pull-ups. Squeeze your shoulder blades together on each pull.

Balance pushing and pulling movements across your week to support good shoulder health and posture.
