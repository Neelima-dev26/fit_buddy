# Thai and Southeast Asian meal ideas
Category: meal
Keywords: thai, vietnamese, southeast asian, noodle soup, curry, rice paper rolls, stir fry
Summary: These cuisines emphasize herbs, vegetables, and light proteins.

Ideas: Vietnamese rice-paper rolls with vegetables and shrimp or tofu, pho or a vegetable noodle soup, Thai vegetable curry with rice, a basil stir-fry with chicken or tofu, papaya salad, or steamed fish with lime and herbs.

Use coconut milk and sauces in moderation, load up on vegetables and herbs, and pair with rice or noodles for a complete meal.
