# How often should I exercise?
Category: general
Keywords: how often, frequency, days per week, weekly, guidelines, minutes
Summary: Adults are advised to do 150 to 300 minutes of moderate activity each week plus strength work on 2 or more days.

Health guidelines suggest 150 to 300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity (or 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous activity) per week, and muscle-strengthening on two or more days. Every minute counts, and you do not need to do it all at once.

For beginners, three sessions a week is a great start, plus easy walks on other days. Add more as it feels manageable, and keep at least one full rest day. If you cannot reach the guidelines, do what you can, because any movement is better than none.
