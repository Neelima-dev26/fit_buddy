# Shoulder health and posture exercises
Category: form
Keywords: shoulders, posture, band pull-aparts, wall slides, rotator cuff, desk
Summary: A few small exercises keep the shoulders and upper back strong and mobile.

Good choices include band pull-aparts, wall slides (sliding your arms up a wall while keeping your back flat), face pulls with a light band, and doorway chest stretches. Do 1 to 2 sets of 10 to 15 repetitions a few times a week.

Combine these with rows, which strengthen the muscles that pull your shoulders back. Take movement breaks if you sit a lot. If you have ongoing shoulder pain or limited movement, see a doctor or physical therapist before pushing through it.
