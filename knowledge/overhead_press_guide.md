# Overhead press: form guide
Category: form
Keywords: shoulder press, overhead press, dumbbell press, shoulders, triceps
Summary: The overhead press builds shoulder and arm strength.

Stand or sit tall holding a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms facing forward or toward each other. Brace your core and press the weights straight overhead until your arms are almost straight, then lower with control.

Keep your ribs down and avoid leaning back or arching your lower back. Start light, since this move feels harder than it looks. Do 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions. If your shoulders feel pinched, try a neutral grip or press at a slight angle, and stop if you feel pain.
