# Smoothies and juices
Category: meal
Keywords: smoothie, juice, blender, protein smoothie, green smoothie, breakfast drink
Summary: Smoothies can be a quick, nutritious option when built well.

Blend a base of milk or plant milk or yogurt with a handful of fruit, a vegetable such as spinach, a source of protein like yogurt, tofu, or nut butter, and a spoonful of oats or seeds for fibre. Add ice for texture.

Whole fruit gives more fibre than juice, so enjoy juice in small amounts. A smoothie can replace a snack or part of a meal, but pair it with food if it needs to hold you over.
