# An evening wind-down routine
Category: wellness

A calm routine tells your body that bedtime is near. Start 30 to 60 minutes before you want to sleep.

Dim the lights, put screens away, and do something relaxing: a warm shower, gentle stretching, reading, journaling, or slow breathing. Prepare for tomorrow, such as laying out clothes, so your mind can settle.

Keep a regular bedtime and wake time. Avoid caffeine late in the day and heavy meals right before bed. This pairs well with a "wind down by 10 pm" habit in your daily checklist.
