# Digital wellbeing and social media
Category: wellness
Keywords: screen time, social media, phone, doomscrolling, notifications, digital detox
Summary: Setting boundaries with screens protects your sleep and mood.

Try turning off nonessential notifications, keeping your phone out of the bedroom, setting app limits, and planning screen-free times, such as meals and the hour before bed. Replace scrolling with a short walk, music, or reading.

Notice how different accounts make you feel, and unfollow ones that leave you feeling worse. Use fitness content as inspiration, not as a standard to compare yourself against.
