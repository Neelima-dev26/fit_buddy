# Getting started with gym machines
Category: workout

Machines guide your movement, which makes them beginner friendly. Good first choices are the leg press, chest press, seated row, lat pulldown, shoulder press, and leg curl.

Adjust the seat and pads so the movement lines up with your joints, start with a light weight, and aim for 10 to 12 repetitions with controlled movement. Rest about 60 to 90 seconds between sets and do 2 sets at first.

Ask a staff member for a quick tour, and wipe down equipment after use. Warm up for 5 to 10 minutes on a bike or treadmill before lifting, and increase the weight slowly once all your reps feel easy.
