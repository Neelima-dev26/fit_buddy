# Side stitches and muscle cramps
Category: general
Keywords: side stitch, cramp, muscle cramp, pain while running, stomach pain exercise
Summary: Slow down, breathe deeply, and these usually pass.

A side stitch is a sharp pain under the ribs during exercise. Slow your pace, take deep belly breaths, and press gently on the area. Avoid large meals and sugary drinks right before exercise, and warm up before you start.

For a muscle cramp, stop, gently stretch and massage the muscle, and drink water. Regular stretching and staying hydrated may help. If cramps are frequent, severe, or come with other symptoms, speak to a doctor.
