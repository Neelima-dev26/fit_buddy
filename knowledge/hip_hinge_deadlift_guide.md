# Hip hinge and Romanian deadlift: form guide
Category: form
Keywords: deadlift, romanian deadlift, hip hinge, hamstrings, glutes, back, form
Summary: The hip hinge teaches you to bend at the hips with a flat back, which protects your spine.

Stand tall with a slight bend in your knees, holding dumbbells in front of your thighs. Push your hips straight back as if closing a door with your bottom, letting the weights slide down your legs while your back stays flat and your chest proud. Stop when you feel a stretch in the back of your thighs, then squeeze your glutes to stand tall.

Keep the weights close to your body and your neck in line with your spine. Practice first with no weight or a light pair, in front of a mirror or from the side on video. Do 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 repetitions. Stop if you feel strain in your lower back.
