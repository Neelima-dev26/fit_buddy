# Four-week beginner progression
Category: workout

A simple way to progress any of the beginner plans is to change one thing at a time.

Week 1: do 2 sets of 8 to 10 repetitions of each exercise, three days per week, and focus on good form. Week 2: add one or two repetitions to each set. Week 3: add a third set to the exercises that feel manageable. Week 4: use slightly heavier weights, a stronger band, or a harder variation, such as moving from knee push-ups to full push-ups.

If you feel very tired or sore, repeat the previous week instead of moving on. After week 4, take an easier recovery week, then start the cycle again at a slightly higher level.
