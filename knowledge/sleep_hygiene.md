# Sleep tips
Category: wellness

Most adults need 7 or more hours of sleep per night, and teenagers generally need 8 to 10 hours. Good sleep supports energy, mood, and recovery from exercise.

Helpful habits include keeping a regular bedtime and wake time, even on weekends, making the bedroom dark, quiet, and cool, and turning off screens 30 to 60 minutes before bed. Try limiting caffeine in the afternoon and evening, and avoid large meals right before bed. A short relaxing routine, such as reading, gentle stretching, or slow breathing, can help you wind down.

Regular daytime activity, especially earlier in the day, often helps sleep. If you struggle with sleep for weeks, or feel very sleepy during the day, talk to a doctor.

Reference: general sleep guidance from public health agencies.
