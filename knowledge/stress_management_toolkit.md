# A stress management toolkit
Category: wellness
Keywords: stress, coping, relaxation, breathing, walking, talk to someone, stress relief
Summary: A mix of quick calming tools and longer-term habits works best.

Quick tools: slow breathing for one minute, a short walk, stretching, a cold glass of water, or stepping outside. Daily habits: regular sleep, movement, balanced meals, time with friends, and limiting caffeine and news.

Break big tasks into small steps, write down worries, and ask for help. If stress feels constant, causes physical symptoms, or interferes with daily life, talk to a doctor or mental health professional.
